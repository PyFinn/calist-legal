7. Data Protection

Information on the processing of personal data in Calist can be found in the separate Privacy Policy at https://pyfinn.github.io/calist-legal/datenschutz.html. The Privacy Policy is not part of this Legal Notice. In particular, the Privacy Policy describes:

  • AI-assisted meal recognition: when a meal is scanned, the meal photo is transmitted – only for the duration of the analysis – to the provider's backend (Supabase) and onward to an AI service (including in the USA, e.g. OpenRouter, Inc. as a sub-processor routing to AI providers such as Google or Anthropic) in order to estimate nutritional values. The provider does not store meal photos on its servers; retention of the image by the downstream AI provider is governed by that provider's own terms. The estimates are approximate, user-editable and do not constitute medical advice;
  • the optional sign-in with Apple ("Sign in with Apple"), where the email address may be a real address or an Apple private-relay (@privaterelay.appleid.com) address depending on the user's choice;
  • the anonymous Supabase session (a random user identifier) created to deliver the meal scan and to enforce a per-user usage quota;
  • AI usage / quota metering data (e.g. user identifier, model, token counts, cost, timestamp – no image and no meal content) stored on the provider's servers (Supabase);
  • subscription management via RevenueCat, Inc. and Apple (payment / card data is collected by Apple and never reaches the provider);
  • profile, body-metric, training, nutrition, running and similar data that is stored exclusively locally on the device and is not transmitted to the provider's servers;
  • international data transfers to the USA and the applicable safeguards, the legal bases, retention periods and the rights of data subjects (including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority).
